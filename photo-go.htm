Gross’ Orchard

This page contains three photo albums from my visits to Gross' Orchards in Bedford, VA (highly recommend). A handful of these photos are on my Instagram page, but the rest didn't make the "Instagram cut". Images are down-scaled so they load faster, but you can click on them to see the full-size image. All images are Copyright © Alan M. Burr and are licensed under CC BY-ND 4.0.

October 2024

October is apple-picking season at Gross' Orchards. This was the first year we went and we enjoyed it thoroughly.

October 2025

About the same as October 2024. Again enjoyed it thoroughly!

July 2026

July is peach-picking season. This was out first time picking peaches! We got caught on an overcast day because this was the first day we could have gone (scheduling and all), and the season would have ended by the time we got back from Rhode Island. The peaches were delicious, of course
